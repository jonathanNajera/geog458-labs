1. Study area and dates

I focused on the Port of Tacoma on May 24, 2026. As someone born in Tacoma, I wanted to map the variety of vessels that travel through Commencement Bay. I chose a Sunday because I was interested in how vessel activity might differ on a weekend. A single day cannot establish what is typical, but it offers a snapshot of activity on that date
2. Interprretation
Throughout the day, I noticed that more vessels in the “Other vessels” category appeared to be heading out. This may reflect recreational boating activity on a Sunday, although one day of data is not enough to confirm that pattern.

The 15-minute playback speed was easiest for me to follow. Cargo vessel activity appeared to begin early in the morning, around 1 a.m.


3. Feature propsoaal for a potential user
Target user: A maritime operations team or private company that uses satellite and AIS data to visualize vessel activity.

I would propose using deck.gl’s PickingInfo feature to let users select a vessel path and view the information available for that feature. This could help operators understand vessel activity in a particular area and support maritime safety and situational awareness.
